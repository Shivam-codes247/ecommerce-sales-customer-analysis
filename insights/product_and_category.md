# Product & Category Insights (Jan 2017 – Aug 2018, delivered orders)
1. 18 of 74 categories (24%) generate 80% of revenue; health_beauty leads with 9.3%.
   At product level, 25.9% of 32,081 products account for 80% — a mild long tail.
2. Unit price, not order count, drives category rank (watches_gifts R$199/unit is #2
   with fewer orders than bed_bath_table at R$93/unit, #3).
3. No single product exceeds 0.5% of revenue; the top 10 total 3.4%.
4. Satisfaction gaps: office_furniture (3.65 avg, 21.7% low reviews) is the clear
   outlier; bed_bath_table (4.00, 16.0% low) matters most by volume. The 10 categories
   that are high-volume and below the 4.16 average hold ~43% of revenue.
5. Neither freight nor lateness explains low category scores. electronics (29.5% freight, 4.12
   score) and food_drink (29.4%, 4.44) show freight is not the driver. bed_bath_table has a
   7.4% late rate, about average, yet scores 4.00. office_furniture is slow (20.5 days) rather
   than late (8.1%). See Operations Insights.

**Recommendations**
- Audit bed_bath_table sellers and product quality (largest volume gap: 16.0% low reviews vs 11.4%
  for health_beauty).
- Investigate office_furniture separately: bulky items, long transit and a 25% freight burden.

*Limitations:* 610 products lack a category (1.3% of revenue); a few category names
contain source typos; satisfaction averages exclude categories with <200 orders.
