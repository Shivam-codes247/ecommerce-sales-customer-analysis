## Sales & Revenue Insights (Jan 2017 – Aug 2018, delivered orders, BRL)

**Headline:** 96,211 orders from 93,104 customers generated R$13.18M in item revenue
(AOV R$137; freight adds R$2.19M, 16.6% of revenue).

1. Growth was volume-led. AOV stayed within R$125–149 while monthly orders grew
   from 750 to ~6,000–7,000. Orders average 1.14 items.
2. Black Friday (24 Nov 2017) produced 1,147 orders, about 4.7x a normal November day.
   The month's +52% jump partly reversed in December, but 2018 settled at a higher baseline.
3. Revenue plateaued after Jan 2018 (R$0.83–0.98M/month) and averaged ~12% lower in
   Jun–Aug than Mar–May. Recommendation: investigate acquisition spend and retention (Phase 5).
4. Revenue is geographically concentrated: SP 38.4%; SP+RJ+MG 63.4%; top 7 states 81.5%.
   Recommendation: test freight subsidies in under-penetrated regions.
5. 77% of orders occur Mon–Fri. Recommendation: schedule promotions on weekdays.

*Limitations:* Aug 2018 slightly understated (undelivered recent orders); remote-state AOVs rest on small samples.
