# Operations & Payment Insights (Jan 2017 – Aug 2018, delivered orders)
1. Median delivery takes 10 days (p90: 23) against an average promise of 24.3 days;
   6.8% of orders arrive after the promised date (6,531 orders).
2. Lateness is strongly associated with lower satisfaction: avg review score falls from
   4.29 (on time or early) to 3.29 (1–3 days late), 2.10 (4–7 days late), and 1.67
   (8–14 days late). Low-score reviews (1–2) are 9.2% for on-time/early orders,
   32.1% for orders 1–3 days late, and 67.7% for orders 4–7 days late.
3. Late rates spiked in peak/stress months: 12.4% (Nov 2017), 14.1% (Feb 2018),
   19.0% (Mar 2018), versus ~3–4% in normal months. Performance recovered from Apr 2018.
4. Rio de Janeiro (13.3% of revenue) has a 12.1% late rate vs 4.5% in São Paulo and a
   3.97 average score vs 4.25. Several Northeast states have 12–22% late rates (BA, CE, PI, SE, MA, AL).
5. Category satisfaction is not explained by lateness: bed_bath_table has an average 7.4% late
   rate but a 4.00 score, and late rates across 43 categories (3.6–11.8%) show no clear link to
   scores. office_furniture is slow (20.5 days) rather than late (8.1%).
6. Remote northern states (AM, AP, AC, RO) take 19–27 days but have <4% late and 4.1–4.2
   scores: honest delivery promises matter more than speed (small samples).
7. Credit cards carry 78.5% of paid value, boleto 18.0%. Average card payment rises from
   R$96 (single payment) to R$332 (7–10 installments), an association, not proof of cause.

**Recommendations**
- Send proactive delay notices and recovery offers in the 1–7 days-late window, where
  the score drop is steepest but not yet at the floor.
- Prioritize carrier/seller performance in RJ and the Northeast; set a late-rate target
  (e.g. <6%) for those states.
- Add peak-season capacity planning (Black Friday and Feb–Mar): staff and cap promise
  dates when volume spikes.
- Test installment messaging on high-ticket categories (A/B test; do not assume causation).
- Investigate office_furniture (bulky items, long transit, 25% freight) and bed_bath_table
  sellers separately from logistics.

*Limitations:* late = delivered on a later calendar date than estimated; 8 delivered
orders without a delivery date excluded from delivery metrics; Aug 2018 delivery times may
be flattered by not-yet-delivered orders; remote-state samples are small.
